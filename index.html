Star Princess · Inside Passage

Ketchikan · Endicott Arm / Dawes Glacier · Juneau · Skagway · Victoria BC. Keep the kid on deck as much as possible — wildlife happens when you're just standing and watching.
Port schedule

Embark Seattle
Departs afternoon. Sail out through Puget Sound into the Inside Passage.
Sun June 29
At sea · Inside Passage
Channel narrows, mountains rise from the water. Binoculars on deck. Watch for orcas, humpbacks, bald eagles.
Mon June 30
Ketchikan
Creek Street boardwalk over salmon-run creek. Totem Bight State Park. Great Alaskan Lumberjack Show (kid-favorite). Fresh halibut dockside.

Morning: glacier calving from deck. Afternoon: Mt. Roberts Tramway, Mendenhall Glacier, whale watching.
Wed July 2
Skagway
White Pass & Yukon Railway — book ahead, narrow gauge to 2,865ft through mountain passes. Gold Rush main street walk. Gold panning for the kid.

At sea · southbound
Rest day on the ship. Dome pool. Watch the passage in reverse.
Fri July 4
Victoria, BC
Butchart Gardens (30 min from downtown, spectacular in July). Inner Harbour waterfront walk. Optional: Fairmont Empress high tea.
Sat July 5

Collect luggage. Drive I-5 north ~90 min. Salish Sea expedition begins.

Stromer Rd, Bellingham
July 5–6
5166 Stromer Rd, Bellingham WA 98226
📍 Open in Maps
Rural acreage just outside the city. One easy afternoon, early night. Tomorrow drive north to Blaine base camp.
Activities
Whatcom Falls Park 📍 Nav
4 miles of easy rainforest trails inside city limits. 1930s WPA stone footbridge over rushing falls. 1.5 hrs casual pace — perfect post-cruise stretch.
Galbraith Mountain MTB 📍 Nav
World-class singletrack, 65+ miles of trails. Green/blue trails manageable for a 9-year-old on a rental. Rent bikes at Kulshan Cycles.
Wildcat Cove · Larrabee State Park
Kayak rental — paddle alongside honeycomb sandstone cliffs with San Juan Island views. Half-day paddle.
Eat
Hokkai Ramen & Sushi
Rich tonkotsu or spicy miso. Family-run strip mall spot. Perfect first PNW dinner.
The Sandwich Odyssey
Massive house-made subs on scratch-baked bread. Great lunch spot.
Boomers Drive-In
Classic car-hop, thick milkshakes, cheap reliable burgers.
Boundary Bay Brewery
Casual family-friendly brewpub. Solid food, good pints.

Merle Pl, Blaine / Birch Bay
July 6–13
8154 Merle Pl, Blaine WA 98230
📍 Open in Maps
Reimagined beach cabin, golf cart district, steps from tidal flats. Your longest phase — build in 2–3 true rest days. Tidal flat walk at low tide is a daily option for the kid.
🏃Activities
⌄
Semiahmoo Spit Trail 📍 Nav
Flat 0.8-mile crushed-rock path into the bay. Harbor seals, herons, eagle nests. Easy morning walk anytime.
SUP / Kayak · Semiahmoo Bay
Shallow, warm, calm — ideal for family rental. Paddle the international boundary line from the water. Book ahead (Semiahmoo Resort). Check tide chart night before.
Tidal Flat Walk · Birch Bay
Walk at low tide — crabs, shells, herons. Check tide chart the night before. The 9-year-old will be occupied for hours.
🍴Eat
⌄
Drayton Harbor Oyster Co. 📍 Nav
Waterfront shack on the Blaine docks. Oysters grilled in garlic butter, seafood tacos. Zero pretense. Mon–Thu opens at noon, Fri–Sun 8am.
The Washingtonian Bistro
Cozy neighborhood anchor. Massive breakfast plates and giant comfort sandwiches.
Edaleen Dairy Parlor
Massive hand-scooped cones of local Whatcom County ice cream. Non-negotiable kid stop.
Everybody's Brewing · Ferndale
20 min south. Casual brewpub, great burgers and pizza, perfect family energy.

F Ave, Anacortes
July 13–16
1113 F Ave, Anacortes WA 98221
📍 Open in Maps
Old Town split-entry home. Walk to marinas and ferries. Most activity-dense phase — Whidbey Island, Deception Pass, Penn Cove, and Fidalgo Bay all within reach.
🏃Activities
⌄
Tommy Thompson Rail Trail 📍 Nav
Flat paved 3.3 miles following old rail shoreline to a trestle bridge over Fidalgo Bay. Rent bikes locally or walk it. Great first-afternoon activity.
Deception Pass Bridge 📍 Nav
Walk the pedestrian spans over the twin suspension bridge. Watch tidal water roar through the rock gorge below. Trails lead down to beach on both sides.
❗ Kayak Bowman Bay ONLY — main pass has dangerous tidal currents
Ebey's Landing Ridge Trail · Whidbey 📍 Nav
5.6-mile loop. Past 19th-century farmsteads, up a windswept bluff with Olympic Mountain views, down to a cobblestone driftwood beach. Budget 3 hrs. Moderate — bluff climb is short and steep but the kid can handle it.
Penn Cove Kayak · Coupeville
Very calm protected water. Paddle past the mussel rafts, watch for harbor seals. Ideal family rental — easy for a 9-year-old. Rent from the Coupeville boat ramp.
Washington Park Loop Drive
Free 2.2-mile one-way road through old growth to rocky Sound shoreline. 30 minutes. Great easy afternoon add-on.
🍴Eat
⌄
Toby's Tavern · Coupeville 📍 Nav
Historic pub directly over Penn Cove. Penn Cove mussels steamed in beer, award-winning clam chowder. Creaky floors, no pretense. Opens 11am daily.
Gere-a-Deli · Anacortes
Downtown landmark in a beautiful old brick building. Giant deli subs, scratch soups, big cake slabs.
Boondocker's · Anacortes
Thick milkshakes, garlic fries, good burgers right off the water.
Chuckanut Manor or Oyster Bar
On Chuckanut Drive en route from Blaine. Both sit on the water — fresh oysters, casual atmosphere. Perfect mid-drive stop on arrival day.
Cap Sante Waterfront Grille
Casual waterfront, good clam chowder. Or grab fresh fish from marina docks and cook at the house.
$1,049 · 7 nights · private waterfront
Naomi Cottage, Bremerton
July 16–23
4938 Naomi Ave, Bremerton WA 98310
📍 Open in Maps
Private waterfront backyard, host-provided kayaks, direct launch ramp down to the narrows. Your deepest saltwater immersion. End the trip slow. Dawn paddles from the backyard are the closing image of the whole trip.
🏃Activities
⌄
Backyard Kayak Launch
Host-provided kayaks, private ramp. Paddle the narrows anytime. Dawn paddles best — calm water, low boat traffic. Explore further each session as you learn the tides.
Illahee State Park Old-Growth 📍 Nav
5 min from cottage. Moss-draped ravine trails to one of WA's largest ancient Douglas Firs, then out onto a driftwood Puget Sound beach. Opens 8am. Discovery Pass required.
Bremerton Naval Museum
Free. The 9-year-old will love the destroyer and submarine exhibits. Good easy afternoon option.
🍴Eat
⌄
Boat Shed Restaurant 📍 Nav
Outdoor deck cantilevered over the saltwater narrows. Award-winning Manila clam broth, local fish, pub burgers as boats pass by. Opens 11am daily.
Sluys Poulsbo Bakery 📍 Nav
30 min north. Viking Cups + cinnamon rolls the size of your face. Opens 5am — arrive before 9am or expect a long line.
Tizley's Europub · Poulsbo
Casual European pub food, good burgers, kid-friendly. Good lunch option on a Poulsbo day.
Hood Canal spot prawns
In season in July — find them at a local dock vendor or seafood counter. Cook at the cottage on the back deck. Worth the effort.
Pike Place Market · Seattle
On Seattle ferry day — bread bowl chowder, Piroshky Piroshky, Beecher's cheese curds. Graze your way through.
